One surveyor carries the golden tag glow. Touch the other surveyor to pass it across. A short cooldown prevents it bouncing straight back. If the tagged surveyor throws a cube and hits the other player, the tag transfers at range.

Tap F, right-click, RB or THROW for a normal cube. Hold the fire control and release to launch one large charged cube that explodes into smaller cubes. A direct charged hit launches the other player into the air. With the violet explosive power, firing into the ground near your own feet gives you a smaller blast-jump too. Cubes now travel faster than a sprinting surveyor. If you collect the violet crystal, every cube becomes dramatically faster with larger explosions and a much bigger spray of fragments. Cubes can hit either player and stick to them, as well as terrain, trees, towers and temporary steps. They glitter and vanish after 30 seconds.

Tower roofs are solid. Reach the very top of a tower to awaken it: its colours shift and it emits a mysterious signal.

Only two crystals are active in the loop at a time. They float high above clearings, so build temporary stone steps to reach them. Red grants super speed, green launches you high enough to clear the towers, blue grants flight-like jumping, violet grants extremely fast long-range explosive cubes, yellow shrinks you to one-third size, and pink triples your size. Each power lasts 60 seconds. The crystal changes type and reappears elsewhere when collected, and a timed overlay confirms your active powers.

Press E or X to place a wide step ahead. B or Y toggles the preview. Steps also vanish after 30 seconds.

Coloured surveyor NPCs wander each environment and chatter when you pass nearby. Charged and explosive blasts can throw them into the air as well. The main menu offers the original forest, a ruined city, a high-tower traversal map and a mountain map.

Touchscreen: left side of your view moves, right side looks. Use BUILD, PLACE, THROW and JUMP below.

Vehicle modes: choose Hover Ship Race or Free Flying on the main menu. In race mode both players use larger low-poly ships and must pass through numbered glowing checkpoint gates in order for three laps. Floating vehicle crystals grant Turbo, Lift, Agility or Overdrive for one minute. In free flying there are no checkpoints, but vehicle crystals still appear. W/S or the left stick controls thrust and braking, A/D turns, mouse or right stick steers and climbs, and Shift / trigger-style sprint input boosts speed.
